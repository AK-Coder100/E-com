Ruby Garments Udyog Enterprises / RB Garment Enterprises

🏢 Corporate Overview

Ruby Wardrobe is the flagship e-commerce direct-to-consumer initiative by Ruby Garments Udyog Enterprises. Headquartered at Townhall, Gorakhpur - 273001, Uttar Pradesh, we specialize in the design, weaving, bio-washing, and precision manufacturing of high-grade knitted and woven apparel.

👑 Leadership & Vision

Founded and spearheaded by Mr. Pappu Singh, Ruby Garments Udyog Enterprises was built on the foundation of bringing genuine 240+ GSM combed cotton streetwear, structured pique polo t-shirts, and durable selvedge denims directly to Indian consumers without predatory retail markups.
